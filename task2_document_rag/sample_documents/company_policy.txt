ACME ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONS — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & OPERATIONAL POLICIES
Document Version: 4.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
================================================================================

1. ANNUAL LEAVE & TIME OFF POLICY
All full-time employees accrue 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year, accruing at 1.66 days per month. Unused PTO up to a maximum of 5 days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, expiring on March 31. Beyond 5 days, unused time off is forfeited unless an exception is approved by HR.

2. REMOTE WORK & HOME OFFICE REIMBURSEMENT
Employees working remotely at least 3 days per week are entitled to a one-time home office setup stipend of $1,000 for purchasing an ergonomic desk, chair, or external display. Additionally, a monthly Internet & Connectivity reimbursement of $75 is disbursed automatically through payroll. To qualify, remote workers must maintain an Internet speed of at least 100 Mbps download and 20 Mbps upload.

3. TRAVEL & BUSINESS EXPENSE POLICY
Business travel must be approved by the Department Director at least 14 days in advance. Domestic flights under 5 hours must be booked in economy class. Daily meal allowances are capped at $85 per day ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $40 dinner). Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the travel date. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable.

4. HEALTH & WELLNESS BENEFITS
Health insurance coverage commences on the first day of the month following the hire date. The company covers 90% of employee healthcare premiums and 75% of dependent premiums. Employees also receive a $500 annual fitness wellness allowance for gym memberships, fitness classes, or athletic equipment.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT & WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION
ACME maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding discrimination, sexual harassment, and retaliation. Reports may be filed anonymously via the ACME Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-ETHX) or via email to compliance@acme-solutions.internal. All investigations are completed within 14 business days.
